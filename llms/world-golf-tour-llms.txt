# World Golf Tour

> World Golf Tour (WGT Golf), operated by World Golf Tour, LLC out of San Francisco, is a free-to-play online golf simulation launched in 2008 that recreates more than twenty real championship courses using photorealistic imagery, played by over 15 million players on web, iOS, and Android. A Battery Ventures portfolio company; it publishes no public developer API surface.
